03 · Positioning

Інновації, народжені відвагою

VALOR Defence Innovation Center turns battlefield experience into deployable technology. We sit between the people who fight and the people who build: engineers, researchers, and serving soldiers in one loop, on the shortest path from a problem seen at the front to a system in the field.

Everything in this identity follows from that: it must look like engineering, not marketing. Restrained where the work is serious, unmistakably Ukrainian where it matters.

We sound
Precise. Plain. No triumphalism, no euphemism.
We show
Hardware, people, evidence — not stock imagery.
We avoid
Weapon fetishism, gradients, hero-speak.
Held high, not swung.
Defence, not conquest.
04 · The mark

Traced, not drawn

The mark is a halftone screen laid over a pixel-accurate trace of the Motherland Monument in Kyiv. Nothing is stylised by hand: the silhouette is the statue's real contour, so sword, shield and robe keep the monument's own proportions.

1 · Contour
Every pixel kept, no redrawn curves — bust cut at the torso
2 · Screen
5px dot grid, radius from coverage; tryzub knocked out
Why halftone
The dot screen is how a press renders a cast relief. It keeps the monument legible without turning it into a war graphic.
The tryzub reads as absence of ink rather than an applied symbol — five plain bars, no ornament, so it survives every reproduction.
One trace, four cuts, no redraws.

06 · Clear space & minimum size

Give it air, don't shrink it past the screen

Clear space = x

08 · Misuse

Six things that break it

The mark carries a national monument. Distortion isn't a style choice here — it reads as carelessness.

✕
Stretch it
Scale proportionally.
✕
Rotate it
The figure stands upright.
✕
Recolour it
Palette colours only.
✕
Add effects
No shadows, bevels, glows.
✕
Low contrast
Never green on mid green.
✕
Halftone too small
Under 28px use the solid.
