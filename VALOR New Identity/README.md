## Non-negotiables
- Halftone above 28px; below that, the solid cut. The dot screen closes up under 28px.
- Clear space on all four sides = the width of the shield.
- Never green-900 on green-800. Never mint body text on paper (2.1:1).
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour, or add shadows/bevels/glows.
- Colour ratio roughly 60 deep green / 26 paper / 9 mist / 5 mint.

## Provenance
The mark is a pixel-accurate trace of the Motherland Monument, Kyiv, screened as a halftone; the tryzub is knocked out of the dot field. The monument is public; the trace and its treatment are original work owned by VALOR.

Need EPS, PDF or CMYK? Those require a print-specific colour build, not a conversion of these RGB masters — request them from the brand owner.
