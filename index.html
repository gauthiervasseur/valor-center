Why Now

Advantage now belongs to whoever learns fastest.

Capability cycles built for a stable world are mismatched to a battlefield where tactics, EW, and autonomy evolve on a week-to-week cadence.

The Problem

  • Capability cycles are too slow for contested EMS, autonomy, EW & AI-enabled operations
  • Innovation is fragmented across nations, primes, startups, and labs
  • Startups struggle to cross the chasm into procurement, integration, and scale
  • Allies need repeatable pathways from validated battlefield insight to fielded, interoperable capability

Why Now

  • Ukraine provides real battlefield expertise, validated datasets, and rapid prototyping cycles
  • SRI brings scientific rigor, world-class labs, and 75+ years of defense partnerships
  • A multinational engine can align NATO/allied problem statements with builders who iterate at battlefield tempo
  • The moment demands institutions built around absorption and learning speed — not just invention
